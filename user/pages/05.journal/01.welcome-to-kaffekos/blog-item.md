We are delighted to welcome you to **Kaffekos**, a Norwegian-inspired coffee house in the heart of Lahore.

Our idea is simple: great coffee, warm bakes and the feeling Norwegians call *kos*. Come in, find a comfortable seat and let us look after you.

We will share news, new menu items and events right here, so check back often, and follow us on Instagram [@kaffekospakistan](https://www.instagram.com/kaffekospakistan/).

*Velkommen!*
