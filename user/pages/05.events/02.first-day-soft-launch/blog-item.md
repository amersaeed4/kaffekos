Today was the day: **Kaffekos opened its doors for the very first time.**

We kept it relaxed on purpose. No big show, just a soft launch to get the coffee flowing, try things out and welcome our first guests in for a cup. Thank you to everyone who dropped by, grabbed a drink and made our first day feel like *kos* from the very start.

The chess board and a round of Quick Puck came out too, and the corners of the café started to feel exactly the way we hoped: warm, lively and a little bit competitive.
