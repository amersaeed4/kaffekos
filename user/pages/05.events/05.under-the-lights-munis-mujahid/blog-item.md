## What a night.

Last Friday, **9 October**, Kaffekos came alive with music.

Under an open sky, with the garden softly lit and our sign glowing behind him, **Munis Mujahid** picked up his guitar and filled Grand Park with song. Guests settled onto cushions and bean bags on the lawn, cups in hand, and for a few hours nobody was in a hurry to be anywhere else.

That is exactly the feeling we dreamed of when we opened our doors: good coffee, good company and the kind of evening that turns into a memory. The Norwegians call it *kos*. Friday night, it had a soundtrack.

### Special thanks to Munis Mujahid

Thank you, **Munis Mujahid**, for choosing Kaffekos as your stage. Your voice, your guitar and the warmth you brought to the garden made the night what it was. We are so grateful, and we would love to have you back.

### And thank you, all of you

To everyone who came, sat on the grass, sang along and shared the night with us: you are the reason we do this. Thank you for making a Friday evening at Kaffekos so special.

### Want more nights like this?

*Velkommen, and see you at the next one!*
