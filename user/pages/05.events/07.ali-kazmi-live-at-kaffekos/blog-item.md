## A Saturday we won't forget.

On **Saturday 17 October**, Kaffekos turned into a live music night.

With our sign glowing, blue lights on the stage and a tree full of colourful ribbons behind him, **Ali Kazmi** took the stage and gave the garden its Saturday soundtrack. Friends, squads and solo guests settled onto bean bags and cushions, phones came up for the best moments, and for a few hours nobody was in a hurry to be anywhere else.

Inside, the counter kept the drinks and bakes coming, and a few of our youngest guests found their own entertainment on the tables. That mix of music, games and good company is exactly what we hoped Kaffekos would be. The Norwegians call it *kos*.

### Special thanks to Ali Kazmi

Thank you, **Ali Kazmi**, for your music and for choosing Kaffekos as your stage. You turned a Saturday into a memory, and we would love to have you back.

### And thank you, all of you

To everyone who came, sang along, filmed, clapped and shared the night with us: you are the reason we do this. Thank you for making it so special.

### Want more nights like this?

*Velkommen, and see you at the next one!*
